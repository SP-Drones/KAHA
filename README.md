**KAHA** is a lightweight, race-focused 5-inch FPV frame designed by **[Southern Performance Drones](https://www.spdrones.co.nz/)**.

This repository contains the open-source components of the KAHA platform, including the arm and locking plate CAD files, along with printable accessories and documentation.

> The complete KAHA frame kit and prints can be purchased from the Southern Performance Drones store.

## Specifications & Features

### Standard Configuration

| Component | Specification |
|----------|---------------|
| Arm Thickness | **6 mm** |
| Locking Plate Thickness | **5 mm** |
| Electronics Mounting | 20×20 mm / 30.5×30.5 mm / TBS Gorilla Mounting |
| Top Plate Mounting | 20×20 mm / 25.5×25.5 mm for VTX |
| Camera Compatibility | HDZero Nano / Analog Nano |

### Features

* Reinforced arm geometry
* Flexible electronics mounting
* Easy individual arm replacement
* Easy-access stack screws
* Available with 5 mm or 6 mm arms

> **Note:** The KAHA frame ships with **6 mm arms** and **5 mm locking plates**. 5 mm arms are available and use the same geometry, just with reduced thickness.

## Repository Contents

### Arms

The replaceable arm design used by the KAHA frame.

Included formats:

* DXF
* STEP

### Locking Plates

The locking plates that secure the arms to the frame.

Included formats:

* DXF
* STEP

### 3D Printable Parts

This repository contains printable accessories including:

* Camera mounts
* Antenna mounts
* Aero covers
* Crash flip fins
* Additional accessories

All printable parts are provided as STL and STEP files.

#### Need Something Else?

If the included mounts don't suit your build, feel free to open an Issue or contact Southern Performance Drones.

Additional camera mounts, camera angles, antenna mounts, and other printable accessories can be designed and added to the repository.

#### Included Camera Mounts

The repository currently includes camera mounts for:

* HDZero Nano 90
* HDZero Nano 90 V2
* Foxeer Predator Nano V5

The Foxeer Predator Nano V5 mount is also compatible with several other nano-sized cameras, although fitment may vary.

> ## Why are only some parts open source?
>
> The KAHA arm and locking plate are derived from the OpenRacer project and are therefore released under the GNU GPL v3, along with any modifications made to them.
>
> The remainder of the frame, including the top plate, bottom plate, mid plate, and other original components - was designed independently by Southern Performance Drones and is not part of this repository.
>
> This repository exists to comply with the GPL while also giving the community access to the printable accessories and supporting documentation used with the KAHA frame.
